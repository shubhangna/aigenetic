The 90% of calls that aren't clinical

Most calls to a clinic are logistics

Your receptionist spends the day answering the same nine questions. Algenetic takes them all, in the caller's language, and leaves you the ones that need a human.

Books the slot

Checks live availability per doctor and per room, offers the next two options, confirms in one turn.

"Kal shaam ka koi time hai?"

Reschedules and cancels

Frees the slot instantly and offers it to your waitlist before anyone else calls.

"Aaj nahi aa paaunga, agle hafte kar dijiye."

Quotes fees and timings

Consultation, follow-up window, which days you do procedures, whether you're at the hospital that morning.

"Root canal ka kitna charge hai?"

Gives directions

Landmark-based, the way people actually navigate. Drops a pin on WhatsApp.

"Clinic metro station se kitna door hai?"

Reports and readiness

Tells the caller whether their report is ready for pickup — status only, never the contents.

"Blood test ki report aa gayi kya?"

Screens the reps

Pharma and equipment reps get your visiting policy instead of your morning.

"Sir se 5 minute mil sakte hain?"
Hard limits, set by us, not by prompt

What it will never do

Your name is on the board outside. These three refusals are built into the product and cannot be turned on, by you or by us.

  • No diagnosis, no adviceSymptoms are logged for you and the caller is offered the earliest slot. Nothing more.
  • No medicine names, no dosagesNot even repeats of what you prescribed last visit. Refills go to you.
  • No results read over the phone"Ready for collection" is the whole answer. Values stay in the file.

And one thing it always does

If a caller describes chest pain, breathlessness, heavy bleeding, a seizure, or an unresponsive patient, the assistant stops booking mid-sentence, reads out the nearest emergency number, and rings your on-duty mobile until someone picks up.
