Before you get started

A few practical questions.

What does Front Desk as a Service include?

Aigenetic brings AI calls, WhatsApp chat and updates, appointment/calendar booking and follow-ups together as one managed front-desk service. Your approved rules define what it handles and when your team steps in.

Can customers start on WhatsApp instead?

Customers can enquire through WhatsApp as well as by phone. We map the relevant answers, booking steps and human handoffs to your business during setup.

Which calendar can we connect?

We confirm your calendar provider, availability rules, booking permissions and rescheduling process before setup. No specific calendar integration is assumed until that conversation.

How are reminders and follow-ups controlled?

Review channel consent, message templates, timing, retry limits and stop rules with your team. The exact workflow and escalation path are agreed before launch.

Can we keep our business number?

We check your current phone and WhatsApp setup, including forwarding, number provisioning and account eligibility. No number change or caller-ID capability is assumed until that check.

What does 24×7 cover?

The service is positioned for always-on AI handling of calls, WhatsApp enquiries, bookings and follow-ups. Human support hours, calendar availability, escalation ownership and outage procedures are agreed separately; 24×7 does not mean round-the-clock human staffing.

What happens when a customer needs a person?

Requests outside the approved scope follow agreed handoff rules. Your team reviews how calls, chats and booking issues are escalated before going live.
