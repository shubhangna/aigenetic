01Overview

Aigenetic (OPC) Pvt Ltd is committed to customer satisfaction. This Refund Policy outlines the circumstances under which refunds are provided for our AI phone assistant services.

Important: Our services are custom-configured for each client, and refund eligibility depends on the service stage and reason for dissatisfaction.

02Setup Fee Refund

One-Time Setup Fee: ₹10,000

✓ Eligible for Refund:

  • Full refund if requested within 24 hours of payment (before setup begins)
  • 50% refund if setup is in progress but service not activated

✗ Not Eligible for Refund:

  • After service activation (phone number assigned and AI configured)
  • After first customer call is handled by the AI
  • After 7 days from payment date

03Monthly Subscription Fee Refund

Monthly Plans (Flex/Pro): ₹4,999 - ₹9,999

✓ Eligible for Refund:

  • Pro-rated refund if service downtime exceeds 5% in a month (excluding planned maintenance)
  • Full refund if we fail to activate service within promised 72-hour window
  • Pro-rated refund for unused days if we terminate service due to our fault

✗ Not Eligible for Refund:

  • If client cancels mid-month (no pro-rating for voluntary cancellation)
  • Dissatisfaction with AI performance (we offer free optimization instead)
  • Changes in business requirements or strategy
  • Months where subscription was already consumed

04Per-Minute Usage Charges Refund

Call Charges: ₹6 per minute

✓ Eligible for Refund:

  • Billing errors or duplicate charges (refunded within 7 days of notification)
  • System errors causing incorrect call handling (verified through logs)
  • Calls where AI completely failed to respond (technical issue on our end)

✗ Not Eligible for Refund:

  • Calls where AI attempted to help but customer was dissatisfied
  • Wrong numbers or spam calls received (industry standard)
  • Call count disputes (verified through telecom logs)
  • Customer miscommunication or unclear requests

05Money-Back Guarantee (First Month)

7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

  • Available only for first-time customers on Flex or Pro plans
  • Request must be made within 7 days of service activation
  • Requires detailed feedback on why service didn't meet expectations
  • Refunds: Monthly fee + usage charges (setup fee not refunded)
  • Limit: Maximum 50 calls handled during trial period

Note: This guarantee is void if you violate our Terms of Service or use the service fraudulently.

06Refund Request Process

How to Request a Refund:

  1. Email connect@aigenetic.in with "Refund Request" in subject line
  2. Include:
    • Your business name and registered phone number
    • Invoice number and payment details
    • Reason for refund request (detailed explanation)
    • Supporting evidence (if applicable)
  3. We review requests within 3-5 business days
  4. If approved, refunds processed within 7-10 business days

Refund Methods:

  • Original payment method (bank transfer, UPI, credit card)
  • Account credit for future services (if preferred)
  • Processing time: 7-10 business days from approval

07Service Credit (Alternative to Refund)

In some cases, we offer service credits instead of refunds:

  • Performance Issues: Free extra month if AI accuracy drops below 85%
  • Downtime: Service credit equal to 2x downtime duration
  • Missed Commitments: Credit for delayed features or integrations
  • Goodwill Gestures: Credits for reported bugs or improvement suggestions

Service credits have no cash value and must be used within 12 months.

08Non-Refundable Items

The following are never refundable under any circumstances:

  • Third-party charges (telecom, SMS, WhatsApp API fees)
  • Custom development work beyond standard service
  • Training sessions or consultations already delivered
  • Services consumed during notice period after cancellation
  • Penalties or late fees incurred due to non-payment

09Chargebacks and Disputes

Important: Please contact us before initiating a chargeback with your bank.

  • We're committed to resolving disputes directly and fairly
  • Chargebacks result in immediate service suspension
  • If chargeback is reversed, a ₹2,000 administrative fee applies
  • Fraudulent chargebacks may result in legal action

10Cancellation vs. Refund

Cancellation Policy:

  • You can cancel anytime with 30 days' notice
  • No refunds for current month's subscription (already consumed)
  • Outstanding usage charges must be paid
  • After cancellation, data retained for 90 days then deleted

11Questions and Support

If you're unhappy with our service, please reach out before requesting a refund. We offer:

  • Free AI optimization and reconfiguration
  • Custom conversation flow adjustments
  • Hands-on training and troubleshooting
  • Plan downgrades or feature modifications

We're committed to making Aigenetic work for your business!
